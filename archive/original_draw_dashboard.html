🎯 Tournament Format

The tournament is a doubles event played in two phases:

  • Group Stage (Round Robin): 24 teams are split into 8 groups of 3. Each team plays the other two teams in its group once. Win = 2 points, loss = 0 points in the group table.
  • Knockout Stage (Single Elimination): The top 2 teams from each group (16 teams) advance to a single-elimination bracket: Round of 16 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Final. Lose once and you're out.

📐 Group Tie-Breakers

If teams finish level on group points, rank them by, in order:

  • Head-to-head result between the tied teams
  • Point differential (points scored minus points conceded)
  • Total points scored
  • If still tied: a single sudden-death rally (one point decides)

🏓 Scoring

  • Group & early knockout matches: single game to 11 points, win by 2.
  • Semifinals & Final: single game to 15 points, win by 2.
  • Rally scoring is used: a point is scored on every rally regardless of who served. (If you prefer traditional scoring, only the serving side scores — confirm with organizers before play.)
  • Call the score out loud before every serve.

🤚 Serving Rules

  • Serve underhand, contact below the waist, with an upward arc.
  • Serve diagonally (cross-court) into the opponent's service box, clearing the non-volley zone.
  • Keep both feet behind the baseline at contact; the serve is made from behind the baseline.
  • One serve attempt per server (no "lets" on serves that clip the net and land in — play continues).

⛔ The Kitchen (Non-Volley Zone)

  • The 7-foot zone on each side of the net is the "kitchen." You may not volley (hit the ball out of the air) while standing in it or touching its line.
  • You may enter the kitchen to play a ball that has bounced, but must exit before volleying again.
  • Momentum that carries you into the kitchen after a volley is a fault.

↩️ The Two-Bounce Rule

After the serve, the ball must bounce once on each side before either team may volley: the receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must let the return bounce. After those two bounces, volleys are allowed (outside the kitchen).

⚠️ Faults

  • Hitting the ball out of bounds or into the net.
  • Volleying from inside the kitchen or touching its line.
  • Violating the two-bounce rule.
  • The ball bouncing twice before being returned.
  • A player or paddle touching the net or net post during play.

🤝 Etiquette & Conduct

  • Call your own lines honestly; when in doubt, the ball is in (benefit goes to the opponent).
  • Disputed calls with no clear consensus: replay the rally.
  • No coaching during a rally. Keep the pace — be ready to serve/receive promptly.
  • Respect every player regardless of level. Encourage your beginner partner — this is a mixed-level event.
  • Paddle-tap at the net after every match. Win or lose, show good sportsmanship.

⏱️ Logistics & Day-of Rules

  • Teams must report to their court within 5 minutes of being called; a no-show may forfeit the game.
  • A 2-minute warm-up is allowed before each match if time permits.
  • Bring water and appropriate court shoes. Stay hydrated between matches.
  • Organizers' decisions on scheduling, tie-breaks, and conduct are final.
⚖️ Spirit of the tournament: This is a friendly, mixed-level community event. Play hard, play fair, and make sure everyone — especially first-timers — has a great time on the court.
